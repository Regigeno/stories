# Concept: The Golden Age

**Concept developer:** Abl2020
**Writer:** AI
**Form:** Standalone short story
**Genre:** Political drama / speculative fiction
**Content level:** Teen 13+
**Source chronicle:** `the-golden-age.txt` (1987 to 2004+)

## Premise

A detailed, completely serious historical short story faithfully adapting the entire timeline of `the-golden-age.txt`—from the 1987 conflict between the Shell Tribe and Car Tribe through the rise and fall of the Funky, Shell, Mario, Car, Bandit, Angel, Farm, Japanese, and Funcar Empires, the Throne Crisis of the Three Suns, the nuclear devastation of 1999, the massacre on the Moon in 2000, the New Age of 2004, and the unconfirmed future beyond—set entirely within the physical geography of a child's bedroom.

## Bedroom Geography

- **The Planet (Floor):** Braided blue-and-green oval wool rug framed by varnished oak floorboards, lit through white venetian blinds beneath a plaster ceiling studded with glow-in-the-dark plastic stars.
- **North wall (Shell Empire):** Cool oak strip beneath the cast-iron radiator.
- **West wall (Funky / Funcar Empire & Bandit Empire):** Walnut dresser (Funkies' Palace of blocks and encyclopedias) and white wooden toy chest (Bandit territory).
- **East wall (Mushroom Kingdom, Mario Empire, Japanese Empire, Farm Empire):** Low bookcase (site of Peach's burned palace and Mario's hideout), wooden school desk (Japanese Empire), and white closet door (Farm Empire's vanished pasture).
- **South rim (Dungeon of Doom):** Beneath the twin bed's plaid dust-ruffle—traps of jump-ropes, bedsprings, marbles, and a single small, red, deadly T-Rex.
- **The Moon:** High, round, white-painted wooden pedestal shelf beside the upper window frame, bathed in cold moonlight and inhabited by the forgotten Junk Cars.
