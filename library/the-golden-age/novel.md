Between the braided wool rug, the walnut dresser, and the plaid dust-ruffle of a child's twin bed, seventeen years of history unfold in deadly earnest.

Beginning in 1987 with the unexplained border war between the Shell Tribe and the Car Tribe, a bedroom floor becomes the stage for regicide, alliances forged and broken, a dark underworld beneath the bedframe, the discovery of science and nuclear fire, a shattered royal succession, an exodus to the Moon, and the uneasy recovery of a world waiting for its next war.
