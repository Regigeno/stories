# Outline: Feeding Hour

Eighteen chapters of at least 2,000 words each. Chapters 1 to 3 are drafted (about 9,200 words). Aim for 2,200 to 2,600 words in the remaining fifteen, for a total near 45,000. Timeline runs from Saturday 12:41 p.m. to Sunday dawn.

**Survivors:** Arjun, Priya, Imogen. Everyone else with a name dies. If a death must be cut for pacing, never save anyone beyond these three.

## Act I: Admission (Chapters 1 to 5)

**1. Admission** (Priya, 12:41 p.m.) [DRAFTED]
Priya and Arjun arrive for his ninth birthday. Small details seed the mystery: the closed Reptile House, a hum underfoot, delivery trucks that do not belong. They join Eddie's tiger feeding talk on Taiga Trail. A tremor, and Katya turns toward the green door. The lights go out at 1:52.

**2. Fail-Open** (Vale, 1:10 p.m.) [DRAFTED]
The investor demo. The cooling plant trips, the doors fail open, and Teague orders the site-seal and refuses to alert the zoo. Calloway defies him and takes Vale toward the east stair. Duchess reaches the elephant barn hatch at 1:55. Seeds: the 18:00 feed tone on Bao's schedule, Atlas as the only Jurassic specimen, and Jazz.

**3. Old Girl** (Mags, 1:54 p.m.) [DRAFTED]
Mags hides in the feed room as Duchess breaks through and nearly finds her. Imani's trumpet draws Duchess outside. Duchess steps into the sun with no kill yet. Gerry herds the crowd. Imani plants herself between Duchess and the people, and the lions come down off the kopje.

**4. The Okavango Run** (Theo, 2:00 p.m.)
Hippo Harbor Cruises with eleven passengers, including Rosalind and her daughter Jess. The hippos behave strangely. The lagoon goes quiet, the crocodiles scatter, and something large moves in the drain culvert. The Spinosaurus's sail breaks the surface and the boat takes a hit. Phones are dead, so Theo cannot call anyone.

**5. Glass** (Priya, 2:04 p.m.)
The tunnel is dark and Eddie is keeping 30 people calm. The green steel door in the yard bursts and Jazz enters. Katya meets her. Jazz leaps onto the glass roof and it fails. The first human kill in the book happens in the tunnel (the woman with the coffee cup from chapter 1). Arjun names the animal. Eddie opens the keeper door.

## Act II: Theaters (Chapters 6 to 14)

**6. The Pride** (animal chapter: Zuberi, Sabi, Imani, 2:07 p.m.)
The lions and the elephant meet Duchess on the open Plains. The pride tries to test and flank her, and Duchess kills them methodically. Zuberi dies. Amara tears out Duchess's right eye before she dies. Imani sets a tusk into Duchess's flank but is crushed and mortally wounded. Zola and Pip retreat into the wrecked barn. Duchess limps off toward the central plaza.

**7. Gideon's Water** (Theo, 2:12 p.m.)
The boat is caught between the Spinosaurus, Old Nile and the crocodiles, and the bull hippo. Rosalind triages in the middle of it. Jess goes into the water and Rosalind goes after her. Theo steers into the narrows. Six of the eleven passengers die. Five reach the pier.

**8. A Pack of One** (Priya, 2:20 p.m.)
Katya and Jazz fight in the yard. Eddie gets Priya, Arjun, and six others into the keepers' building. Katya mortally wounds Jazz and dies. Eddie holds the door and dies. Jazz, hurt and in pain, hunts the rest. Priya and Arjun escape through the green door into the Ring.

**9. The Long Neck** (Imogen, 2:28 p.m.)
Imogen climbs out of a maintenance hatch near the Giraffe Overlook, tracking Atlas by his collar. Dr. Ode has a dart rifle and six frightened visitors on the platform. Atlas has wandered into Kofi's paddock and Kofi is attacking. Imogen runs in to protect Atlas. Ode darts Kofi. Atlas panics and swings his tail, killing Kofi and sweeping Dr. Ode off the platform. Imogen is left with Ode's radio and rifle, and Atlas.

**10. Dead Air** (Calloway, 2:40 p.m.)
Calloway comes back down from the east stair. Bao is still in the control room, under guard by Teague's deputy, Ruiz. Calloway takes the room, and he and Bao patch the lab's paging system into the keeper radio channel and tell the zoo the truth: what is loose, where it came from, and that the gates are locked on purpose. Teague fights back, and Calloway learns about Sublevel 5 and the Ash Protocol.

**11. Imani** (Mags, 2:55 p.m.)
In the barn, Imani is dying, with Zola and Pip close. Mags hears Calloway's broadcast on the keeper channel. Bev's radio goes silent. Gerry reveals the rail cut under the turntable pit. Mags, Gerry, and about 40 visitors form a plan. Imani dies.

**12. Last Boat** (Theo, 3:15 p.m.)
The five survivors are on the pier. Spino finishes off Gideon and Old Nile and turns on the pier. Theo draws the Spinosaurus back into the water. Rosalind and Jess die. Theo dies. Spino leaves the lagoon wounded, heading inland.

**13. The Quiet Ring** (Priya, 3:50 p.m.)
In the Ring, Priya and Arjun find the elephant barn's open hatch and Mags's group coming down into it. Gerry shows Arjun the turntable. Jazz, half-blind, follows their scent. Three people die in a short, ugly fight. The rail cut's far gate is jammed and needs time and tools. The group will wait out the afternoon in the pit.

**14. The Handler** (Imogen, 4:30 p.m.)
Imogen has hidden Atlas in the ruined Reptile House. She reaches Bao on the lab channel and learns about the 18:00 feed tone and what Teague plans to do with it. She realizes Atlas will walk home to his death at six. She decides to go back below.

## Act III: Feeding Hour (Chapters 15 to 18)

**15. Feeding Hour** (Calloway, 5:30 p.m.)
Calloway and Teague, with two opposite plans for the tone. Teague wants to bring every animal home and recapture them. Calloway wants to let them come, and then bring the hill down. Bao dies. Calloway forces Teague's abort code and arms the Ash Protocol, with the collapse timed after the tone. Teague escapes up the Sublevel 5 lift with a cryogenic case. Calloway, badly wounded, stays at the console.

**16. Turntable** (Mags, 5:55 p.m.)
The tone begins. Duchess, half-blind, turns for home, and her home runs through the elephant barn. Mags holds the barn door against Duchess with Zola so the group can drop into the pit. Mags and Zola die. Gerry takes the group into the rail cut.

**17. The Cut** (Priya, 6:02 p.m.)
In the rail cut, Gerry leads Priya, Arjun, and about twenty people toward the harbor gate. The tone calls Jazz down the Ring, and the Ring crosses the cut. Gerry sacrifices himself to hold the old signal door. Most of the group dies. Priya and Arjun reach the harbor fence as the first charges go off behind them.

**18. Ash** (Imogen, 6:05 p.m. to dawn)
Imogen follows Atlas below as the tone sounds. Spino, Duchess, Jazz, and Atlas converge on Paddock Three. Imogen has the abort code from Bao and the chance to stop the collapse, which would save Atlas and let the others live. She chooses not to. The hill comes down. Every dinosaur dies. The power failure opens the gates, as fail-open doors do. At dawn Imogen climbs from the vent shaft and finds Teague, dead, with a cryogenic case marked SMILODON. She takes it. Priya and Arjun watch the sun rise from the harbor fence, alive. Last image: the case.

## Continuity checkpoints

- Times in chapter headers must be consistent with the timeline above.
- Four free dinosaurs only: Duchess, Spino, Jazz, Atlas.
- The feed tone sounds at 18:00 and runs on its own battery.
- Cell service is dead from 1:53 p.m. Keeper radios work throughout, but nobody on them knows what is happening until Chapter 10.
- Duchess loses her right eye in Chapter 6.
- Jazz loses an eye in Chapter 8, so by Chapter 13 she is half-blind.
- The gates stay locked until the power dies in Chapter 18.
- Atlas is about 20 feet and under 2 tons.
