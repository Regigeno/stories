# Outline: Feeding Hour

Eighteen chapters of at least 2,000 words each, for about 40,000 words. Timeline runs from Saturday 12:41 p.m. to Sunday dawn.

## Act I: Admission (Chapters 1 to 5)

**1. Admission** (Priya, 12:41 p.m.)
Priya and Arjun arrive for his ninth birthday. Small world details seed the mystery: the closed Reptile House, a hum underfoot, delivery trucks that do not belong. They join Eddie's tiger feeding talk on Taiga Trail. A tremor makes the water in a cup ripple and Katya turn toward the back door.

**2. Fail-Open** (Vale, 1:10 p.m.)
Underground. The investor demonstration begins and the cooling plant trips. Vale's safety warning about fail-open doors comes true. Teague orders the site-seal. Vale and Calloway are cut off from the zoo. The first paddocks open.

**3. Old Girl** (Mags, 1:55 p.m.)
Imani senses the vibrations before anyone else. The elephant barn's hatch buckles. R-1 climbs out of the barn and onto the Plains. The lions go silent. The crowd at the moat railing does not understand what it is looking at.

**4. The Okavango Run** (Theo, 2:00 p.m.)
The boat tour. The hippos behave strangely. Something large moves in the culvert. S-1's sail breaks the surface. The boat takes a hit.

**5. Glass** (Priya, 2:04 p.m.)
The first raptor appears in the enclosure. Katya's cubs are in the back. The glass tunnel holds, then does not. Eddie's first kill call. Priya takes Arjun and runs.

## Act II: Exhibits (Chapters 6 to 13)

**6. The Pride** (animal chapter: Zuberi, Sabi, Imani, 2:07 p.m.)
The lions and the elephant meet R-1 in the open yard. The pride tries to test and flank. Imani positions between R-1 and the calves of the herd in the barn, tries to get the barn door closed with her body. Zuberi is injured, Sabi circles.

**7. Gideon's Water** (Theo, 2:12 p.m.)
The boat is caught between S-1, Old Nile and the crocodiles, and the bull hippo. Rosalind triages. Jess goes into the water. Theo steers into the narrows. The boat reaches the pier at a cost.

**8. Pack Hunters** (Priya, 2:20 p.m.)
The raptors work the Taiga yard, testing the tigers' reach. Katya kills two but is outflanked. Priya, Arjun, and Eddie flee through the keepers' building into the Ring. The pack follows the scent.

**9. The Long Neck** (animal chapter: Atlas, Kofi, with Dr. Ode, 2:30 p.m.)
Atlas, the juvenile Brontosaurus, wanders into the giraffe paddock and finds company. Kofi interprets the stranger as an intruder and attacks. Atlas panics and swings his tail. Dr. Ode has to decide between a dart and a rifle.

**10. Sublevel** (Vale, 2:35 p.m.)
Vale and Calloway reach a stairwell and find the site-seal is deliberately holding the zoo in. She learns from the telemetry that eleven animals are loose. She finds a keeper radio and calls the zoo.

**11. Radio** (Mags, 2:50 p.m.)
The fight on the Plains is over, with losses. Imani is hurt but R-1 is wounded and angry. Mags hears Vale on the keeper radio. Visitors shelter in the elephant barn. Mags decides to help guide the animals away from the people.

**12. White Water** (animal chapter: Nanuq vs U-2, 3:10 p.m.)
U-2 stalks the polar bear's yard as a guest. The bear is displaced but not weak. The fight is in and around the pool. Nanuq wins by water and weight and is badly hurt.

**13. Gideon** (Theo, 3:30 p.m.)
The lagoon fight ends. Gideon is dead, the crocodiles are scattered, and S-1 heads for the Carousel Court, where it senses the largest herd of living things in the zoo. Theo and Rosalind get onto the pier and onto a radio.

## Act III: Convergence (Chapters 14 to 18)

**14. The Ring** (Priya, 4:00 p.m.)
In the service tunnels, Priya, Arjun, and Eddie meet Vale and Calloway. Vale explains Lullaby and the control room's tranquilizer vent. The raptors have found the tunnel.

**15. Carousel Court** (Mags, 4:50 p.m.)
Hundreds of visitors are stranded in the central plaza and education center. R-1 enters from the Plains. S-1 arrives from the lagoon. Mags and the survivors try to lead people out of the plaza through the north service road.

**16. Two Kings** (animal chapter: R-1 and S-1 with Imani, 5:20 p.m.)
The two apex predators meet in the plaza. The fight is brutal, and Imani, injured, intervenes.

**17. Lullaby** (Priya and Vale, 5:45 p.m.)
In the control room, Priya, Vale, and Calloway force the Lullaby vents open. Raptors in the Ring, Katya at the door. Teague is waiting.

**18. After Hours** (Mags, Vale, Priya; dusk to dawn)
Aftermath. The count of survivors and the dead. Teague's confession on the keeper radio's recording. The animals are sedated, then moved. In the last scene, Vale and Priya find that the lift to the unlisted fifth sublevel is open from the inside.

## Continuity checkpoints

- Times in chapter headers must be consistent with the timeline above.
- The Lullaby sedative needs about 12 minutes to take effect on a theropod.
- Eleven loose animals: R-1, S-1, V-1 to V-7, U-2, J-1.
- Keeper radios work only on the zoo channel. Mobile phones are jammed.
